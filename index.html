Privacy Policy

CareerPilot - Resume Builder

Developed by Evolix Technologies Last updated: September 17, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to CareerPilot - Resume Builder ("CareerPilot", "the App", "we", "our", or "us"), developed by Evolix Technologies.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, processed, and protected when you use CareerPilot.

CareerPilot is designed as an offline-first resume building application. You can create, edit, preview, and generate resumes without creating an account.

Google Sign-In and cloud synchronization are optional features provided for users who want to back up or synchronize their resume data across supported devices.

Important: You do not need to create an account or sign in to create and export resumes using CareerPilot.

2. Information You Provide

CareerPilot may process information that you voluntarily enter into your resume.

This information may include:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Address or location
  • Professional title
  • Profile photo
  • Professional summary
  • Employment history
  • Education information
  • Skills
  • Projects
  • Certifications
  • Achievements
  • Languages
  • Interests
  • References
  • Custom resume sections
  • Other information that you choose to include in your resume

3. Offline Resume Data

CareerPilot is designed to work offline.

When you use the App without enabling cloud synchronization, resume information is stored locally on your device.

Local resume information is not intentionally uploaded to our servers unless you choose to sign in and enable cloud synchronization or use another feature that explicitly requires online processing.

If you uninstall the App, clear the App's storage, reset your device, or lose access to your device before enabling synchronization, locally stored resume data may be lost.

4. Google Sign-In

CareerPilot may allow you to optionally sign in using your Google account.

When you choose Google Sign-In, we may receive basic account information provided through Google's authentication service, such as:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your Google account identifier
  • Your profile photo, where available

We use this information to authenticate your account and provide optional cloud synchronization functionality.

CareerPilot does not receive or store your Google account password.

Your use of Google services is also subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

5. Cloud Synchronization

If you choose to sign in and enable synchronization, your resume data may be securely stored using cloud services such as Firebase.

Cloud synchronization may be used to:

  • Back up your resumes
  • Restore resume data
  • Synchronize resumes across supported devices
  • Maintain account-related preferences

Cloud synchronization is optional. You may continue using the resume-building functionality without signing in.

6. Firebase Services

CareerPilot may use services provided by Google Firebase.

Depending on the features enabled in the App, these services may include:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Cloud Firestore
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Firebase Analytics

Firebase may process certain device, application, diagnostic, and technical information as required to provide these services.

The exact information processed depends on the Firebase services enabled in the version of CareerPilot that you use.

7. Notifications

CareerPilot may send notifications relating to application features, resume updates, template availability, product updates, reminders, or other relevant information.

Where required by your device or operating system, CareerPilot will request permission before sending notifications.

You may disable notifications at any time through your device settings.

8. Purchases and Premium Templates

CareerPilot may offer premium resume templates or other paid digital features through Google Play Billing.

Payments are processed by Google Play.

Evolix Technologies does not directly receive or store your full payment card information.

We may receive purchase-related information such as:

  • Product identifier
  • Purchase status
  • Purchase token
  • Transaction-related identifiers
  • Entitlement status

This information may be used to verify purchases, unlock premium content, restore purchases, and prevent unauthorized access to paid features.

9. Resume PDF Generation

CareerPilot allows you to generate resume documents, including PDF files, using information that you provide.

PDF generation may occur locally on your device.

When you choose to save, share, print, or export a resume, the file may be transferred to another application or service selected by you.

Once a resume is shared with another application, person, cloud provider, employer, or service, CareerPilot cannot control how that third party uses the information contained in the document.

10. Information Automatically Collected

Depending on the services enabled within CareerPilot, limited technical information may be collected automatically.

This may include:

  • Device type
  • Operating system version
  • Application version
  • Crash reports
  • Diagnostic information
  • Performance information
  • General usage events
  • Device or application identifiers where permitted

This information may be used to maintain application stability, identify technical problems, improve performance, and understand how application features are used.

11. How We Use Information

Information processed through CareerPilot may be used to:

  • Create and manage resumes
  • Save resume information locally
  • Provide optional cloud synchronization
  • Authenticate users who choose to sign in
  • Generate and export resume documents
  • Provide free and premium templates
  • Verify in-app purchases
  • Restore purchased content
  • Send requested or permitted notifications
  • Improve application stability and performance
  • Diagnose technical issues
  • Protect the App from fraud or abuse
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

12. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed or shared with service providers only when reasonably necessary to operate CareerPilot.

These providers may include:

  • Google Firebase
  • Google Sign-In
  • Google Play Billing
  • Cloud infrastructure and hosting providers
  • Analytics or crash-reporting services where enabled

Information may also be disclosed if required by law, legal process, court order, government request, or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, security, and safety of CareerPilot, Evolix Technologies, our users, or others.

13. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures intended to protect information processed through CareerPilot.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Users are responsible for protecting access to their devices and accounts.

14. Data Retention

Resume information stored locally remains on your device until you delete the resume, clear application data, or uninstall the App, subject to the behavior of your device and operating system.

If cloud synchronization is enabled, synchronized information may be retained while your account remains active or for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and enforce agreements.

15. Account and Data Deletion

Users may delete locally stored resume information from within the App where deletion functionality is available.

Users who sign in and synchronize data may request deletion of their account and associated cloud data.

To request deletion of cloud-stored CareerPilot information, contact:

Email: privacy@evolixtechnologies.com

Please use the subject: CareerPilot Data Deletion Request.

We may need to verify your identity before processing certain deletion requests.

16. Your Choices

Depending on how you use CareerPilot, you may choose to:

  • Use the App without signing in
  • Keep resume data only on your device
  • Enable or disable cloud synchronization
  • Choose which resume sections to include
  • Delete saved resumes
  • Disable notifications through device settings
  • Sign out of your Google account
  • Request deletion of synchronized account data

CareerPilot may integrate with third-party platforms and services.

These third-party services operate according to their own privacy policies and terms.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services you use in connection with CareerPilot.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through CareerPilot in a manner that violates applicable law, please contact us so that we can review and take appropriate action.

Services used by CareerPilot, including cloud infrastructure providers, may process information in countries other than the country in which you reside.

Where applicable, we rely on appropriate safeguards and service provider protections for international data processing.
