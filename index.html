Privacy Policy

CareerPilot - Resume Builder

Developed by Evolix Technologies Last updated: September 17, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to CareerPilot - Resume Builder ("CareerPilot", "the App", "we", "our", or "us"), developed by Evolix Technologies.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, processed, stored, used, and protected when you use CareerPilot.

CareerPilot is designed as an offline-first resume-building application. You can create, edit, customize, preview, and export resumes without creating an account.

Google Sign-In and cloud synchronization are optional features intended for users who want to synchronize or back up their resume information across supported devices.

Important: You do not need to create an account or sign in to create, edit, preview, or export resumes using CareerPilot.

2. Information You Provide

CareerPilot processes information that you voluntarily provide while creating or editing a resume.

Depending on the information you choose to add, this may include:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Address or location
  • Professional title
  • Profile photo
  • Professional summary
  • Employment history
  • Education information
  • Professional and technical skills
  • Projects
  • Certifications
  • Achievements
  • Languages
  • Interests
  • References
  • Custom resume sections
  • Other information that you choose to include in your resume

You control which information and resume sections you choose to provide and include.

3. Offline Resume Data

CareerPilot is designed to provide core resume-building functionality offline.

When you use CareerPilot without enabling cloud synchronization, your resume information is stored locally on your device.

Resume information stored locally is not intentionally uploaded to our cloud infrastructure unless you choose to use a feature that requires cloud processing or synchronization.

If you uninstall the App, clear application storage, reset your device, or lose access to your device without first synchronizing your information, your locally stored resume data may be lost.

4. Optional Google Sign-In

CareerPilot may allow you to optionally sign in using your Google account.

Google Sign-In is not required to create or export a resume.

If you choose to sign in, we may receive basic account information provided by Google, such as:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your Google account identifier
  • Your Google profile photo, where available

This information may be used to authenticate your account and provide optional synchronization features.

CareerPilot does not receive or store your Google account password.

Google Sign-In is also subject to Google's own terms, policies, and privacy practices.

5. Cloud Synchronization

If you choose to sign in and enable synchronization, your resume information may be stored using cloud services, including Google Firebase.

Cloud synchronization may be used to:

  • Back up your resumes
  • Restore synchronized resumes
  • Synchronize resumes across supported devices
  • Store account-related preferences
  • Maintain synchronization status

Cloud synchronization is optional.

6. Firebase Services

CareerPilot may use services provided by Google Firebase to provide selected online functionality.

Depending on the version and configuration of the App, these services may include:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Cloud Firestore
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Firebase Analytics

Firebase may process certain technical, application, device, diagnostic, or usage information as required to provide those services.

The exact data processed depends on which Firebase services are enabled in the version of CareerPilot you use.

7. Notifications

CareerPilot may provide notifications relating to resume reminders, new templates, important application information, feature updates, or other relevant content.

Where required by your operating system, CareerPilot will request permission before sending notifications.

You may disable notifications through your device settings at any time.

8. Premium Templates and In-App Purchases

CareerPilot may provide free and premium resume templates.

Premium templates or other paid digital features may be purchased through Google Play Billing.

Payments are processed by Google Play.

Evolix Technologies does not directly receive or store your complete payment card information.

We may receive limited transaction information required to validate and maintain purchases, including:

  • Product identifier
  • Purchase status
  • Purchase token
  • Transaction identifiers
  • Entitlement status

This information may be used to verify purchases, unlock premium content, restore purchases, and help prevent unauthorized use.

9. Resume PDF Generation and Export

CareerPilot allows you to generate resume documents, including PDF files, using information you provide.

PDF generation may take place locally on your device.

When you choose to save, print, share, or export a resume, your document may be transferred to an application, device location, cloud provider, person, employer, or service selected by you.

Once you share or export a resume outside CareerPilot, we cannot control how the receiving application, service, or recipient processes that information.

10. Information Automatically Collected

Depending on the services enabled within the App, limited technical information may be collected automatically.

This may include:

  • Device type
  • Operating system version
  • Application version
  • Crash information
  • Diagnostic information
  • Performance information
  • General feature usage events
  • Application or device identifiers where permitted

This information may be used to maintain application stability, identify technical issues, improve performance, and understand how application features are used.

11. How We Use Information

Information processed through CareerPilot may be used to:

  • Create and manage resumes
  • Store resume information locally
  • Provide optional cloud synchronization
  • Authenticate users who choose to sign in
  • Generate resume documents
  • Provide PDF export functionality
  • Provide free and premium resume templates
  • Verify and restore in-app purchases
  • Provide notifications where enabled
  • Improve performance and reliability
  • Identify and diagnose technical issues
  • Protect CareerPilot from fraud or abuse
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

12. Sharing of Information

Evolix Technologies does not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers when reasonably necessary to provide CareerPilot functionality.

These service providers may include:

  • Google Firebase
  • Google Sign-In
  • Google Play Billing
  • Cloud infrastructure providers
  • Analytics providers where enabled
  • Crash and diagnostic service providers where enabled

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, legal process, court order, government request, or when reasonably necessary to protect the security, rights, or safety of CareerPilot, Evolix Technologies, our users, or others.

13. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures intended to protect information processed through CareerPilot.

However, no electronic storage system or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device, Google account, and exported resume files.

14. Data Retention

Resume information stored locally generally remains on your device until you delete the resume, clear application data, uninstall CareerPilot, or your operating system removes the data.

If cloud synchronization is enabled, synchronized information may remain stored while your account is active or for as long as reasonably necessary to provide requested services.

Certain information may also be retained where necessary to comply with legal obligations, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, or enforce applicable agreements.

15. Account and Data Deletion

You may delete locally stored resumes using available CareerPilot deletion controls.

Users who choose Google Sign-In and cloud synchronization may also request deletion of synchronized CareerPilot account information.

To request deletion of cloud-stored data, please contact Evolix Technologies.

Email: privacy@evolixtechnologies.com

Subject: CareerPilot Data Deletion Request

We may need to verify your identity before completing certain deletion requests.

16. Your Choices

CareerPilot gives you control over how you use the App.

Depending on available features, you may choose to:

  • Use CareerPilot without signing in
  • Keep resume information only on your device
  • Sign in with Google for optional synchronization
  • Enable or disable selected resume sections
  • Reorder resume sections
  • Delete saved resumes
  • Disable notifications
  • Sign out of your account
  • Request deletion of synchronized data
